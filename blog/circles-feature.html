Your Trusted Circle: How Obserc's Circle System Works

In an emergency, strangers cannot help you the way the right five people can. Obserc's Circle system is built around that truth.

Back to Blog

When something goes wrong, the people you instinctively reach for are not emergency services. They are the handful of people who know you, care about you, and will actually pick up the phone. Obserc's Circle system is built around that fundamental truth.

Your Circle is the core of everything Obserc does. It is a small, private group of trusted contacts who share real-time safety awareness with you. Not followers. Not a public feed. Just the people who need to know when you are okay and when you are not.

What Is a Circle?

A Circle in Obserc is a private safety network of trusted contacts. Think of it as the group you would call at 2am. You choose who is in it: close friends, family members, housemates, a partner. Everyone in the Circle gets a gentle, ongoing picture of each other's safety status.

Circles are not about surveillance. They are about ambient awareness. When you are home safe, your Circle knows. When you are on a night out, they are quietly in the loop. Nobody needs to send a "did you get home okay?" text because Obserc handles that automatically.

"Your Circle is not a feature. It is the foundation that every other Obserc tool is built on."

How to Set Up Your Circle

Setting up your Circle takes about two minutes. Open Obserc, navigate to the Circle tab, and invite contacts directly from your phone. Each person you invite receives a notification and can accept or decline. Once they join, you will both appear in each other's Circles automatically.

You control visibility at all times. You can set whether Circle members can see your live location, your current status, or both. When you need privacy, you can switch to Ghost Mode with a single tap, pausing all sharing without anyone being notified.

What Your Circle Can See

Circle members see a live status feed that updates based on what you are doing in the app. When you activate Night Out Mode, your Circle sees that you are out. When Drive Mode starts, they get a Live Drive Card. When you check in at home, a quiet confirmation appears in their feed.

  • Status updates, Your current activity shared passively with your Circle.
  • Live location, Optional real-time location sharing during active modes like Drive or Night Out.
  • SOS alerts, Immediate notification if you trigger an SOS Beacon, including your exact location.
  • Check-ins, Automatic confirmations when you arrive at a destination safely.

Why Trusted People Matter More Than Strangers

Most safety apps route emergencies to professional dispatch services. That can be vital, but it is not the whole picture. In the majority of situations where someone feels unsafe, what they need is not a dispatcher in another city. They need someone who knows their face, their routine, and their context.

Research consistently shows that people are more likely to seek help and to receive it when they have a warm, personal network behind them. Obserc does not replace emergency services. It layers a trusted human network on top of them, so that the people closest to you are always the first to know. Your Circle is your safety net. Build it well.
