SOS Beacon: Obserc's Most Powerful Emergency Feature Explained

When every second counts, the SOS Beacon cuts through everything. Here is exactly how it works, when to use it, and what happens on your Circle's end.

Back to Blog

Every safety app promises an emergency button. What makes the difference is what happens after you press it. With Obserc's SOS Beacon, the answer is simple: every person in your Circle receives an immediate alert with your live location, and they know exactly what to do because they know you.

The SOS Beacon is the most powerful tool in Obserc, and it is deliberately designed to be simple enough to use under extreme stress. Here is how it works.

How the SOS Beacon Works

The SOS Beacon lives on the main screen of Obserc, always within reach. To send a standard alert to your Circle, you tap the beacon. To escalate to a Critical SOS, you hold it for three seconds. The hold mechanism is intentional, it prevents accidental triggers while ensuring that even in a shaking, panicked hand, a deliberate press will always connect.

When you trigger the SOS Beacon, Obserc immediately broadcasts an emergency alert to every member of your Circle. That alert includes your precise GPS coordinates, a timestamp, and a live-updating location link so your Circle can track where you are in real time as the situation develops.

"The SOS Beacon is the last line of defence, but because your Circle already knows your context, they know exactly how to respond."

What Your Circle Receives

When a beacon fires, every Circle member gets a high-priority push notification that cannot be silenced by Do Not Disturb. The notification includes:

  • Your name and the alert type, Standard SOS or Critical SOS, so they know the urgency level.
  • Your live location, A real-time map link that updates as you move.
  • The time the beacon was triggered, So they know how long ago the situation started.

Your Circle members are not left wondering what to do. Because they are your trusted contacts, they have the context to act: call you, call emergency services, head to your location, or alert others. The beacon does not replace 999 or 911, it gives the people who love you the information they need to mobilise immediately.

SOS Beacon vs a Regular Emergency Call

A standard emergency call is reactive and requires you to speak. The SOS Beacon is silent, instant, and works even if your phone is face-down in a pocket. There is no 30-second hold delay, no need to unlock your phone, and no requirement to explain your situation to a stranger.

Crucially, the SOS Beacon alerts multiple people simultaneously. A phone call reaches one person at a time. Your Circle gets the alert as a group, which means someone in a better position to help might respond first. Geography, availability, and capability all factor in, and your Circle accounts for all of them.

When Should You Use the SOS Beacon?

The SOS Beacon is for situations where you feel your safety is at risk and you need your trusted people to know immediately. This includes being followed, feeling threatened in a public space, a car incident, a sudden medical concern, or any moment where instinct says something is wrong and you need backup.

You do not need to be certain something bad is happening. If it feels serious enough to want someone to know where you are, the beacon is the right call. That is what your Circle is for.
