Ultimate Nightlife Protection

Night Out Mode

When the music's loud and plans change fast, Night Out Mode keeps everyone aligned, visible, and protected without killing the vibe. It’s the ultimate digital wingman.

📡 Find My Squad

Activate a live radar to find exactly where your friends are in real-time. Never lose your group in a crowded club, bar, or festival again.

👀 I Feel Off Button

Not feeling great about a situation? Tap "I Feel Off" to silently alert your active circle that you might need intervention or help soon.

Emergency Response

Your Personal SOS Beacon

Instantly share your live location and an emergency alert with the people you trust most, right when you need them.

  • 🚨
    Standard SOS (Tap)A single tap sends an immediate, silent alert to your active circle with your exact coordinates.
  • ⚠️
    Critical SOS (Hold 3s)Hold the button to escalate. This bypasses Do Not Disturb and alerts all your designated emergency contacts instantly.
  • ⏱️
    10-Second Cancel WindowAccidents happen. If you trigger an SOS by mistake, you have 10 seconds to slide to cancel before any alerts are dispatched.
