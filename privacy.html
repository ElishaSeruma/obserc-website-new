Privacy Policy for Obserc

Effective Date: March 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Obserc ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how Obserc collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Obserc mobile application and related services.

Obserc is designed to support personal safety and social connectivity through trusted networks known as Circles. Because the app includes location sharing and safety alerts, some data is required for the app to function properly.

By using Obserc, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Username
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home location (optional)
  • Emergency Contacts (optional)

This information is used to create and manage your account and enable communication with your trusted network.

2.2 Location Information

Obserc may collect location information to support core safety features such as:

  • SOS Beacon alerts
  • Circle member location visibility
  • Find Your Squad functionality
  • Night Out Mode safety tools

Location data may include:

  • GPS location
  • Approximate location
  • Device location updates while the app is active

Location sharing only occurs with users you have explicitly added to your Circles. You can disable location access at any time through your device settings, although certain safety features may no longer function properly.

2.3 Safety Event Data

When safety features are used, Obserc may collect data related to safety events such as:

  • SOS beacon activations
  • Critical alerts
  • Safety call triggers
  • Circle notifications
  • Activity interactions

This information helps ensure that alerts are delivered correctly and allows users to review safety activity within their Circle.

2.4 Device and Usage Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information including:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Device identifiers
  • Basic diagnostic information

This information helps us maintain service reliability and improve app performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate the Obserc service
  • Enable Circle safety networks
  • Deliver SOS alerts and safety notifications
  • Allow trusted contacts to respond to safety events
  • Improve the performance and reliability of the app
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain the security of the platform

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

4. Sharing of Information

Your information may be shared in the following circumstances:

4.1 With Your Circle Members

Certain information is shared with users you choose to add to your Circles, including:

  • Your username and profile information
  • Your safety alerts
  • Your shared location when safety features are active

You control who can join your Circle.

4.2 Emergency Situations

If you activate certain safety features such as an SOS Beacon or emergency call, your information (including location) may be shared with:

  • Your Circle members
  • Emergency services (when applicable)

This sharing occurs only when safety features are triggered.

4.3 Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party services to operate the platform, such as:

  • Cloud infrastructure providers
  • Notification delivery services
  • Analytics services

These providers are only given access to the information necessary to perform their services.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Security measures may include:

  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Secure cloud infrastructure
  • Access controls and authentication systems

However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the Obserc service.

If you delete your account, your personal information will be removed from our active systems within a reasonable period unless retention is required for legal or operational reasons.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities

Requests may be submitted by contacting us using the information below.

8. Children's Privacy

Obserc is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that personal data from a child has been collected, we will take steps to delete such information.

9. Third-Party Services

Obserc may include links or integrations with third-party services. These services operate under their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their data practices.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, or legal obligations.

When changes are made, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. Continued use of the app after updates indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, you may contact us at:

Email: support@obserc.com
Website: https://obserc.com

Safety Disclaimer

Obserc is designed to support personal safety, but the app cannot guarantee protection in all situations. Users should always rely on their judgment and contact emergency services when necessary.
