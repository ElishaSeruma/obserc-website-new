Privacy & Transparency

How Your Location Is Used

Obserc uses location to help safety features work when they matter most. This includes experiences like SOS Beacon, Circle visibility, Find Your Squad, and Night Out Mode.

The data we use

Depending on the feature you choose to turn on, Obserc may use GPS location, approximate location, and live location updates while the app is active. This helps trusted people in your Circle stay informed during moments when visibility matters.

Who can see it

Location sharing is designed around trust. Your location is never shared publicly. It is only shared with people you have explicitly added to your Circles, and in certain emergency situations it may also be used to support safety alerts and emergency response.

You stay in control

You can change or disable location access at any time in your device settings. Keep in mind that turning it off may limit how some Obserc safety features work. Furthermore, Obserc does not sell personal data to third parties.

In Short

Obserc uses location to power safety features like SOS Beacon, Circle visibility, Find Your Squad, and Night Out Mode. Your location is only shared with people you have explicitly added to your Circles, and you can disable location access at any time in your device settings. Some features may not work properly without it.
